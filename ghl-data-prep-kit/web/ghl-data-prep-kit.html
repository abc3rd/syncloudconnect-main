What each step does

  1. Merge: combines every file you drop into one list.
  2. Map: renames columns like "Cell Phone", "E-mail Address" and "Zip" to GoHighLevel field names, and splits "Full Name" into first and last.
  3. Clean: phones to +1XXXXXXXXXX format, emails lowercased with common typos fixed (gmial.com, .con), names out of ALL CAPS, state names to 2 letters, ZIP codes that lost their leading zero, websites with https://.
  4. Dedupe: merges contacts that share an email or phone, keeping every filled-in field and every tag.
  5. Stamp: adds your tags and lead source so imported contacts drop straight into the right workflow.
  6. Precheck: holds back rows with no valid email or phone, and notes fixable problems on rows that can still import.
  7. Split: cuts the clean list into files under your size limit (GoHighLevel documents 30 MB; raise it if your account allows more), with an optional row cap, and optionally one set per state or tag.

TYRQ score: of all the problems found, the share the kit fixed on its own. 100 means nothing was left for a person to handle.

Command-line version for agencies and bulk jobs: python ghl_prepkit.py run *.csv --outdir out
